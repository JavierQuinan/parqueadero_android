# Code of Conduct

Parking Android is an engineering project. Participation should remain technical, respectful and focused on improving the software and its documentation.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- discuss code, architecture, security and product decisions on their technical merits
- provide actionable review feedback and accept justified technical disagreement
- distinguish verified behavior from assumptions or roadmap proposals
- protect credentials, personal data and private-system information
- use synthetic/demo evidence in public issues, pull requests and screenshots
- respect licenses, attribution and third-party intellectual property

## Unacceptable behavior

The following is not acceptable in project spaces:

- harassment, threats, personal attacks or discriminatory conduct
- publishing personal information or private communications without authorization
- intentionally exposing credentials, vulnerabilities or sensitive operational details
- spam, deceptive claims, fabricated test evidence or deliberately misleading technical statements
- disruption unrelated to the project's engineering work

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and other spaces used to represent or maintain this project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject or remove contributions that violate this policy and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.

Security reports must follow [`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md) rather than public disclosure of exploitable details.
